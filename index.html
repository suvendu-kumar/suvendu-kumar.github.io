Research Overview

Four connected research themes

My work applies machine learning to molecular and biomedical data, with an emphasis on methods that combine complementary data modalities into models that are useful for discovery.

01 Multimodal Molecular AI & Representation Learning

Problem. No single molecular representation - fingerprints, descriptors, or learned graphs - captures everything needed to predict molecular behaviour.

Methods. Hierarchical autoencoders, state-space sequence models, and self-supervised learning to fuse complementary chemical representations into unified embeddings.

Why it matters. Better molecular embeddings improve property and activity prediction, which underpins virtual screening and AI-driven drug discovery.

Representative work: Chemical Dice Integrator (Nature Communications, 2026).

02 Biomedical Machine Learning & Precision Oncology

Problem. Genomic alterations in cancer are difficult to interpret and report consistently at clinical scale.

Methods. Whole-exome sequencing workflows, curated biomedical knowledge bases, and LLM-assisted variant interpretation for structured genomic reporting.

Why it matters. Automated, knowledge-driven interpretation can make precision oncology workflows faster and more reproducible.

Representative work: OncoGen.AI (npj Precision Oncology, 2026); immunotherapy-response prediction at INDX Technology.

03 Genomics, Transcriptomics & Computational Biology

Problem. High-dimensional sequencing data requires rigorous computational analysis to yield interpretable biological signal.

Methods. Bulk and single-cell RNA-seq analysis, mRNA/miRNA integration, variant calling, mutational profiling, and differential expression analysis.

Why it matters. Sound genomic analysis is the foundation for biomarker discovery and mechanistic insight into disease.

Representative work: integrative transcriptomics of non-small cell lung cancer (IIIT-Delhi); genomic characterization at NIPGR.

04 Biomedical Knowledge Integration & Scientific AI

Problem. General-purpose language models lack grounding in structured biomedical knowledge and can drift from domain facts.

Methods. Supervised fine-tuning (LoRA) of domain LLMs on knowledge-graph-derived synthetic QA, with systematic evaluation on biomedical benchmarks.

Why it matters. Knowledge-grounded models are more transparent and reliable for research question-answering and decision support.

Representative work: BioMistral-7B fine-tuning on the EvoAge knowledge graph (ongoing).

Selected Research Projects

Projects

Four projects that best represent my research. Status labels distinguish published work from ongoing research and internal tooling.

Chemical Dice Integrator (CDI)

A multimodal deep-learning framework that integrates complementary molecular representations into unified embeddings for molecular property and QSAR prediction.

Published · Nature Communications 2026
Research question, contribution & methods
Research question
Can complementary molecular representations be fused into a single scalable embedding that outperforms single-modality models on property prediction?
My contribution
Co-first author. Developed the framework combining hierarchical autoencoders with a state-space model for representation distillation; designed the evaluation with 5-fold cross-validation, ablation studies, and QSAR benchmarking; built reusable Python and R interfaces for downstream analysis.
Methods & stack
Python, PyTorch, RDKit, hierarchical autoencoders, state-space models (Mamba/SSM), R.
Evidence & links
Journal article (Nature Communications) · Preprint (bioRxiv)

OncoGen.AI

An end-to-end platform for automated genomic analysis, knowledge-driven variant interpretation, and clinical reporting in precision oncology.

Published · npj Precision Oncology 2026
Research question, contribution & methods
Research question
Can whole-exome analysis, curated biomedical knowledge, and LLM-based interpretation be combined into an automated, structured genomic reporting workflow?
My contribution
Co-first author. Developed computational workflows for whole-exome sequencing analysis and characterization of genomic alterations in cancer, and contributed the LLM-assisted interpretation components that combine curated knowledge with automated reporting.
Methods & stack
Python, WES processing and variant analysis, biomedical knowledge bases, large language models.
Evidence & links
Journal article (npj Precision Oncology)

Knowledge-Graph-Enhanced Biomedical Language Models

Domain-grounding large language models with structured biomedical knowledge, evaluated against standard biomedical QA benchmarks.

Ongoing research · unpublished
Research question, contribution & methods
Research question
Can structured biomedical knowledge be injected into an LLM via fine-tuning without degrading its general biomedical reasoning ability?
My contribution
Fine-tuned BioMistral-7B on the EvoAge aging knowledge graph (1.04B triples, 6 species), using GraphGen for synthetic QA generation and LLaMA-Factory for LoRA supervised fine-tuning. Identified a balanced configuration that minimized graph knowledge loss while avoiding degradation on MedMCQA and PubMedQA; evaluated with the LM Evaluation Harness.
Methods & stack
BioMistral-7B, LLaMA-Factory, LoRA, GraphGen, vLLM/SGLang, LM Evaluation Harness.
Status
Results are part of ongoing doctoral research; a manuscript has not yet been published.

HPC Resource Monitoring for Scientific Computing

An agentless monitoring system for CPU, GPU, and memory usage across distributed HPC nodes, built to keep large-scale scientific workloads observable.

Completed · internal research tool
Problem, contribution & methods
Problem
Large training and analysis workloads run across multiple HPC nodes, but lab-scale clusters often lack lightweight, centralized visibility into resource usage.
My contribution
Designed and implemented an agentless FastAPI/SQLAlchemy system that collects CPU, GPU, and RAM usage from distributed nodes over SSH, with persistent storage and querying for monitoring long-running scientific jobs.
Methods & stack
Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SSH, Linux, HPC workflows.
Status
In use as an internal lab tool; not a published artifact.
