Entry 02

Research

A working record of projects, roughly in the order they were opened.

  1. 2023 — 2026

    Chemical Dice Integrator (CDI)

    Multimodal molecular representation learning

    First-author framework for scalable multimodal molecular representation learning and property prediction, integrating complementary molecular descriptors through hierarchical autoencoders and a state-space model to produce compact molecular representations. Validated with 5-fold cross-validation, ablation studies, and benchmarking for molecular property and QSAR prediction, with reusable Python and R interfaces for downstream analysis.

    Accepted for publication in Nature Communications.

  2. 2023 — 2026

    OncoGen.AI

    Genomic analysis & biomedical AI

    Computational workflows for whole-exome sequencing analysis and characterization of genomic alterations in cancer, combining curated biomedical knowledge with LLM-based analysis for automated interpretation and structured genomic reporting.

  3. 2026

    Knowledge-graph enhanced LLM modeling

    Biomedical systems

    Fine-tuned large language models using biomedical knowledge graphs for domain-grounded representation and reasoning.

  4. 2024

    HPC resource monitoring

    Distributed scientific computing

    An agentless FastAPI / SQLAlchemy system to monitor CPU, GPU, and RAM usage across distributed HPC nodes over SSH, alongside tooling for reliable execution and monitoring of large-scale scientific workloads.
